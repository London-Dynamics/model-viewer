Centurion in the water-pro demo

This page runs threejs-water-pro the way its own demo does: one WebGPU renderer, sunset water units left intact, and the boat scaled by 15. The Ri230 puzzle GLB is 7.56 m long on +X. A −90° yaw puts the bow on +Z before that scale, so the hull is about 113 m in the same world as the demo ship. Calm lowers wave height only. It does not divide the clipmap or wavelengths by 15.

Glass on this GLB is one BLEND material, "Glass", opacity 0.1, depthWrite false. Decals are MASK. Hull only hides the water mesh so the same materials can be compared without the water shader. The windshield view looks through that glass into the cockpit, and the side glass shows the water behind it. model-viewer ld_water still writes buoyancy in parent-local Y and samples a 7.6 m box on the beam axis; use this page until that mixin matches the wrapper used here. Query params: time=sunset|midday, sea=light|calm, view=hero|glass, reference=1. A full 4k sky prefilter did not finish on a software WebGPU adapter; add pmremsize=512 there. A desktop GPU can omit it.

Next boats: generate a Forest River and an Aquila puzzle GLB, measure the AABB, confirm which axis is the bow, then call the same scale-15 placement. Do not reuse the Ri230 sample metres. If their windows use KHR_materials_transmission instead of alpha BLEND, the water depth pass will treat them as opaque until that extension is handled.
