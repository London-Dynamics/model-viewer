Exterior camera from the published configurator. Water is a mixin attribute.

Mixin

Aquila 45 Sport, live attributes

This page sets the same attributes the live Agora exterior preset sets on model-viewer, then calls setCameraControlsMode, setCameraView, and toggleLights(false) after load. The hull is the published puzzle-default GLB. It is not authored with the waterline at y = 0. Add water with the button or ?water=1. That only sets water and water-waterline="0.95". The preset stays the vendor default, blackFlag. ?buoyancy=1 also sets water-buoyancy. Override the draft with ?waterline= metres.
