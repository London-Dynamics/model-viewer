Boats in the water-pro demo

This page runs threejs-water-pro the way its own demo does: one WebGPU renderer, sunset water units left intact, and the boat scaled by 15. Pick a hull with boat=ri245 (default), boat=aquila (alias 45-sport), or boat=demo (the vendored dutch ship). All three share one high starboard-bow hero angle. Distance follows the larger of length and height so the ship's masts stay in frame, with the bow on +Z. Ri245 and Aquila sink so the rub rail meets the lake; the demo ship's origin is already its waterline. Afternoon is the reference clock at 16:45 (Sky Pro latitude 45) with wind 6.7 m/s and a 22 m swell wavelength. This build has no Sky Pro preset and no cloud-coverage control. Sunset is a warm low sun and a deeper sea. Midday is a high sun and a bright lake. Calm lowers wave height only. It does not divide the clipmap by 15.

Aquila windshield glass is KHR_materials_transmission. The page turns those windows into a light alpha blend and leaves them out of the hull mask, so the lake shows through the glass instead of the sand floor. The demo ship has no windshield. Query params: boat=ri245|aquila|45-sport|demo, time=afternoon|sunset|midday, sea=light|calm, view=hero|glass, reference=1. A full 4k sky prefilter did not finish on a software WebGPU adapter; add pmremsize=512 there. A desktop GPU can omit it.

Ri245 requires KHR_draco_mesh_compression. The page attaches DRACOLoader before either load. Aquila's puzzle GLB has no Draco meshes; the same loader still runs. Bounds and the windshield point live in boat-placement.js. Re-measure the AABB if a puzzle is regenerated.
