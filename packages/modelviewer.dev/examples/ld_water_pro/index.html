Boats in the water-pro demo

This page runs threejs-water-pro the way its own demo does: one WebGPU renderer, sunset water units left intact, and the boat scaled by 15. Pick a hull with boat=ri245 (default) or boat=aquila (alias 45-sport). Ri245 MY 2027 is 8.23 m on +X and 3.88 m of beam on Z; a −90° yaw puts the bow on +Z, about 123 m in the demo world. Aquila 45 Sport is 14.18 m on +Z and 4.61 m of beam on X. The bow already faces +Z, so yaw stays 0 and the cat is about 213 m long at the same scale. Calm lowers wave height only. It does not divide the clipmap or wavelengths by 15. Near water stays lake blue: the grazing mirror of the sun and the white deck is softened.

Ri245 window glass is the BLEND material "Glass", opacity 0.1. Aquila windshield-glass is KHR_materials_transmission with an opaque alpha mode, plus one BLEND material GlassClear at opacity 0.2. The water depth pass still treats transmission materials as opaque. Hull only hides the water mesh so the same materials can be compared without the water shader. Query params: boat=ri245|aquila|45-sport, time=sunset|midday, sea=light|calm, view=hero|glass, reference=1. A full 4k sky prefilter did not finish on a software WebGPU adapter; add pmremsize=512 there. A desktop GPU can omit it.

Ri245 requires KHR_draco_mesh_compression. The page attaches DRACOLoader before either load. Aquila's puzzle GLB has no Draco meshes; the same loader still runs. Bounds and the windshield point live in boat-placement.js. Re-measure the AABB if a puzzle is regenerated.
