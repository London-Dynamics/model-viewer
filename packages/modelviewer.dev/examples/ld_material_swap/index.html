LD Material Swap by London Dynamics. The two body boxes share one red material with the trim. Swapping the bodies gives them their own material and leaves the trim on the original.

applyMaterialSwaps points glTF meshes at a material file. Mesh names come from the glTF, and a later call reuses the owned material.
